HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to the title

01

Pick your weight class

Register with your fighter name and wallet. Enter as many weight classes and sports as you like – but every fighter appears only once per ranking.

02

Play and collect points

Games are entered with WBU Coin. Everything you achieve is converted into ranking points – coins never appear in the ranking, only points.

03

Be No. 1 on Title Day

Whoever leads the ranking of a weight class at 23:59 UTC on 30 June or 31 December is crowned WBU World Champion. Then all points go back to zero and a new season starts – your coins stay yours.

WORLD RANKING

Just like the real ranking

Each weight class has its reigning World Champion at the top, followed by ranks 1 to 15 – exactly like a professional boxing ranking. Expand to Top 30, Top 45 and beyond.

The Top 16 earn with every fight

50 % of every game entry in a weight class is paid out in WBU Coin to its Champion and ranks 1–15. They earn crypto, never points – so the race stays open.

THE PRIZE

A real WBU World Champion belt

Not a badge. Not an NFT. The same official WBU belt our professional champions fight for – shipped to the door of every new champion.

One belt for every weight class – and later for every WBU sport. The real belt is shipped when at least 200 rounds were played in that weight class during the season; otherwise the champion receives the title and a digital belt in the Hall of Fame. Between Title Days the current leader holds the interim title. After each Title Day all points reset to zero and a new season begins; your coins stay in your wallet and the final standings move into the WBU Hall of Fame.
