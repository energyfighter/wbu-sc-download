HOW IT WORKS

Ten rounds. Three weight classes of difficulty.

Every question has four answers – placed in the four corners of the ring. Questions get harder round by round, and harder rounds score more.

ROUNDS 1–6

Flyweight

Easy questions · 1 point each

ROUNDS 7–14

Middleweight

Medium questions · 2 points each

ROUNDS 15–20

Heavyweight

Hard questions · 3 points each

PERFECT FIGHT

All 20 right = 45 points

40 points plus a +5 bonus for a perfect fight.

Questions come from the official WBU rules, the WBU coaching licence and boxing history. You can never lose points – your goal is to beat your own record.

🏆 New all-time high score = 2 free games: beat the best score ever played in the Fight Quiz and you get 2 free games.
